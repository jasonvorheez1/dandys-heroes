=====FILE: Characters/Astro/Skillset.txt=====

Role: Support Controller / Enchanter
Tier: S Tier

Astro is a gentle, reserved support whose abilities revolve around rest, dreams, and protecting teammates. In Dandy's World, he restores stamina and watches over exhausted allies, while his personality emphasizes calming others and quietly helping from the background. This kit transforms those themes into powerful battlefield support without making him an overwhelming carry.

Dream Pulse

White Skill
Damage Type: Magic Damage

Passive: Astro radiates a peaceful aura. Allies gradually recover Energy every second while they remain near him.

Whenever an ally falls below 40% Health, Astro immediately grants them a small Heal and a brief increase to Damage Reduction. This effect has a short cooldown per ally.

Active: Astro releases a wave of dream energy across the battlefield.

All allies recover a large amount of Energy, are Healed, and have one negative status effect removed.

Enemies caught in the wave take Magic Damage and become Drowsy, reducing their Attack Speed and Skill Haste for several seconds.

Peaceful Slumber

Green Skill
Damage Type: Crowd Control

Astro places the enemy with the highest Energy into a magical sleep.

Sleeping enemies are unable to act for several seconds. Taking heavy damage awakens them early.

When the target awakens, they deal reduced damage for several seconds and lose a portion of their Energy.

Falling Stars

Blue Skill
Damage Type: Magic Damage

Small stars rain from above, striking random enemies several times.

Each impact deals Magic Damage.

If a target is Drowsy or Asleep, the stars always prioritize them and deal increased damage.

Every strike slightly restores Health to the weakest ally.

Well Rested

Purple Skill
Damage Type: Passive

Astro's soothing presence strengthens the team throughout battle.

Allies gain increased Healing Received and Energy Generation.

Whenever an ally is Healed to full Health, their next skill deals increased damage and ignores a portion of the target's Magic Defense.

Astro also regenerates Energy faster than normal.

Endless Night

Red Skill
Damage Type: Max HP
Magic Defense
Healing

At the start of battle, Astro blankets the battlefield in a peaceful night for several seconds.

During this time:

Allies gradually recover Health and Energy every second.
Enemy Energy generation is reduced.
Enemies affected by Drowsy cannot gain Attack Speed bonuses.
Sleeping enemies remain asleep for longer.

Whenever Astro casts a skill, the ally with the lowest Health immediately gains a Shield and becomes immune to Crowd Control for a short duration.

If an ally would be defeated while Astro is alive, they instead survive with a small amount of Health, instantly recover Energy, and become Untargetable for a brief moment. This effect can only occur once per ally each battle.

Why S Tier?

Astro is one of the strongest dedicated supports in the game. His constant Energy generation, healing, cleansing, and defensive utility allow his team to outlast prolonged fights, while Sleep provides reliable crowd control against dangerous enemies. Unlike SS-tier supports that can completely dictate the pace of a battle, Astro focuses on enabling his teammates rather than winning fights on his own, making him an excellent but balanced S-tier support.

Bassie

Role: Support / DPS
Tier: A Tier

Bassie always keeps her basket stocked with useful supplies, believing that being prepared is the best way to help her friends. She eagerly shares whatever she has with her teammates, and when her basket is overflowing, she turns the remaining supplies into surprisingly effective attacks.

Active: Bassie throws every remaining Supply at the largest group of enemies. Each Supply deals Physical Damage and briefly reduces the target's Attack. Enemies hit by multiple Supplies become Exposed, causing them to take increased damage from allies for several seconds.

Basket Toss	Blue	Physical Damage	Bassie hurls her basket into the enemy team before quickly catching it. The basket bursts open, scattering eggs, ribbons, and supplies across the battlefield.

Always Prepared	Purple	Max HP
Attack	Bassie's careful planning pays off.

Her maximum Supply storage increases by 2.

Whenever an ally consumes a Supply, Bassie permanently gains a small amount of Attack for the remainder of the battle.

Whenever Bassie reaches maximum Supplies, her next damaging skill ignores a portion of the target's Armor.

Why A Tier?

Bassie is a proactive support that rewards preparation. She constantly keeps her basket stocked, automatically helping struggling teammates while converting any extra Supplies into offensive pressure. Unlike Cocoa, whose BonBons continuously accelerate an entire team, Bassie focuses on resource management, deciding whether her Supplies are used to rescue allies or fuel her own attacks.

Her versatility makes her valuable in many team compositions, but because her strongest effects depend on maintaining a healthy stock of Supplies, she lacks the overwhelming consistency and tempo control that define SS-tier supports. She excels as a flexible hybrid who always has something useful ready.

Bobette Carolynne

Role: Defensive Support / Survival Tank
Tier: B Tier

Bobette is a cheerful holiday Toon who loves preparing gifts and making others feel safe. Her strength is not overwhelming enemies, but ensuring her team always has a second chance. Through wrapping supplies, protective presents, and her own durability, Bobette turns losing situations into recoverable ones.

She is not as disruptive as Scraps or as powerful of a support as Astro, but she provides consistent protection and team stability.

Precious Packaging

White Skill
Damage Type: Shield

Passive: Bobette keeps extra wrapping supplies ready.

Whenever Bobette takes a large amount of damage, she gains a stack of Packed Protection.

Each stack increases her Armor slightly.

At maximum stacks, her next Shield ability becomes stronger.

Active: Bobette quickly wraps herself inside a large gift package.

While wrapped:

Bobette cannot attack or move.
Bobette takes greatly reduced damage.
Bobette cannot be affected by Crowd Control.

When the package opens:

Bobette restores Health.
Nearby allies gain a Shield.
Nearby enemies are pushed away and briefly slowed.
Holiday Spirit

Green Skill
Damage Type: Healing / Buff

Bobette shares her positive energy with her teammates.

All allies gain Festive Cheer:

Increased Movement Speed.
Increased Energy Generation.
Increased Healing Received.

Whenever an ally uses their ultimate ability while Festive Cheer is active, Bobette creates a small Shield for them.

The effect becomes stronger for every nearby ally.

Wrapped Surprise

Blue Skill
Damage Type: Physical Damage

Bobette throws decorated presents at enemies.

Each present deals Physical Damage and applies Wrapped.

Wrapped enemies:

Have reduced Attack Speed.
Have reduced Skill Haste.
Generate less Energy.

If a Wrapped enemy is hit by another Bobette ability, the wrapping becomes tighter, briefly Rooting them.

Ornament Protection

Purple Skill
Damage Type: Max HP / Armor

Bobette becomes stronger when protecting others.

Whenever a nearby ally loses Health, Bobette gains a stack of Festive Guard.

Each stack increases:

Armor.
Magic Defense.
Shield strength.

At maximum stacks, Bobette shares Festive Guard with nearby allies.

Whenever Bobette gains a Shield, allies with Festive Guard gain a smaller Shield as well.

The Perfect Present

Red Skill
Damage Type: Max HP / Armor / Healing

Before battle begins, Bobette prepares a special present for her team.

The ally with the lowest Max HP receives Holiday Favor:

Increased Max HP.
Damage Reduction.
Increased Healing Received.

If that ally would be defeated, Holiday Favor opens instead.

They survive with a portion of their Health and become protected for several seconds.

After the present opens:

Bobette gains:

Increased Armor.
Increased Shield strength.
Increased Healing.

However, Bobette's damage is reduced because she focuses entirely on protecting her friends.

Why B Tier?

Bobette is a reliable defensive support who specializes in preventing defeats rather than winning fights quickly. Her Shields, emergency protection, and team buffs make her valuable in long battles, especially with fragile damage dealers.

However, she lacks the battlefield control of higher-tier tanks and does not provide enough offensive support to compete with stronger characters. She cannot force enemies into bad positions like Scraps or completely empower a team like Astro.

B Tier: A dependable protector who keeps the team alive through preparation and care.

=====FILE: Characters/Boxten/Skillset.txt=====

Boxten

Role: DPS / Consistent Attacker
Tier: D Tier

Boxten is a quiet and reserved music box Toon who prefers staying around familiar faces. His actual ability, Wind-Up, increases his Extraction Speed depending on how many Toons are alive, making him a team-oriented character rather than a fighter.

For an RPG auto battler adaptation, Boxten becomes a steady but unimpressive DPS. His music box design gives him a unique rhythm-based fighting style, but he lacks the explosive damage, survivability, or utility needed to compete with stronger damage dealers. His strength comes from consistency, not power.

Why D Tier?

Boxten is a reliable damage dealer, but he struggles to stand out. His rhythm mechanic rewards consistent attacking, but it takes time to build and does not provide the same explosive payoff as stronger DPS characters.

Compared to Rudie, who can snowball through speed and momentum, or other high-tier carries who have stronger burst or team effects, Boxten is simply too dependent on maintaining his setup.

His greatest strength is reliability. He does not completely fail, but he rarely dominates.

D Tier: A steady music-box fighter who can keep a rhythm going, but lacks the impact needed to compete with stronger DPS characters.

Cocoa

Role: Support / Utility / Buffer
Tier: SS Tier

Cocoa is an endlessly optimistic chocolate bunny who believes everyone deserves help, even if they never ask for it. She constantly prepares BonBon candies for her teammates, rewarding proactive play and ensuring her allies always have the resources to keep fighting. Rather than restoring large amounts of Health, Cocoa excels at accelerating her entire team through buffs, Energy, and cooldown reduction.

Name	Rarity	Damage Type	Description
BonBon Delivery	White	Buff	Passive: Every few seconds, Cocoa prepares a BonBon. She can store up to 5 BonBons.

Whenever an ally uses a skill, Cocoa immediately throws them a BonBon if one is available. Each ally can only receive a BonBon this way once every several seconds.

If that ally already has Sugar Rush, the BonBon restores Energy and extends the existing buff instead of wasting the delivery.

Active: Cocoa throws BonBons to every nearby ally. Allies that receive one gain Sugar Rush for several seconds.

Sugar Rush increases Attack Speed, Movement Speed, Skill Haste, and Energy Generation.

If an ally already has Sugar Rush, consuming another BonBon instead restores Energy and extends Sugar Rush's duration.
Sharing is Caring	Green	Shield / Cleanse	Cocoa refuses to leave anyone behind.

Whenever an ally gains Sugar Rush, they also gain a Shield based on Cocoa's Max HP.

If the ally is already Shielded, they instead restore Health over several seconds.

Shielded allies also restore a small amount of Energy when this healing effect begins.

Allies below half HP receive a stronger Shield. Casting this skill applies BonBon Blessing to every ally. BonBon Blessing cleanses one negative status when received and improves Crowd Control Resistance.
Sweet Surprise	Blue	Physical Damage	Cocoa tosses a large chocolate treat into the largest group of enemies. The treat bursts apart, dealing Physical Damage and covering enemies in sticky chocolate for several seconds.

Chocolate-covered enemies have reduced Attack Speed and Skill Haste.

Whenever an ally attacks a chocolate-covered enemy, they restore a small amount of Energy and briefly gain Movement Speed. Cocoa only rewards each attacker once every short interval so the effect stays useful without becoming infinite.
Always Prepared	Purple	Energy	Cocoa is always ready with another treat.

Whenever an ally consumes a BonBon, Cocoa has a chance to immediately prepare another one.

Whenever an ally under Sugar Rush personally defeats an enemy, Cocoa restores Energy, reduces the cooldown of BonBon Delivery, and prepares another BonBon.

Cocoa's maximum BonBon storage is increased by 2.
A Basket for Everyone	Red	Max HP
Shield	Cocoa begins every battle with maximum BonBons prepared.

Sugar Rush now also grants increased Critical Chance and Crowd Control Resistance.

Whenever an ally falls below a percentage of their Max HP, Cocoa immediately gives them a BonBon without consuming one from storage. This effect has a cooldown for each ally.

While every living ally is affected by Sugar Rush, all allies gain increased Skill Haste, generate additional Energy, and cannot have their positive buffs removed or stolen.

This completed delivery cycle also pulses Energy to the whole team and grants temporary protection against buff removal.

Whenever Cocoa prepares a BonBon while already at maximum storage, the excess BonBon is automatically delivered to the ally with the lowest HP.
Why SS Tier?

Cocoa is one of the strongest supports because she constantly improves the tempo of her entire team. Instead of waiting for the perfect moment, she is always preparing BonBons, rewarding allies for using skills and staying active. Her buffs create a positive feedback loop where teammates gain more Energy, use more abilities, receive more BonBons, and continue accelerating throughout the fight.

She contributes very little direct damage and has modest personal defenses, but no other support can maintain such consistent team-wide buffs and resource generation over the course of a long battle. Her ability to keep allies empowered almost indefinitely makes her a cornerstone of many team compositions.

SS Tier: A proactive support whose endless supply of BonBons keeps her entire team fighting faster, stronger, and longer.


=====FILE: Characters/Connie/Skillset.txt=====

Connie is a ghost-themed Toon built around stealth, survival, and escaping dangerous situations. Her biggest strengths are her extremely high stealth, her ability to temporarily become invisible with Haunting Escape, and her ability to avoid being targeted rather than simply overpowering enemies. In an RPG auto battler, she would translate extremely well into a high-value evasive support unit who manipulates enemy targeting and protects allies through ghostly tricks.

Additional Stat Boosts:
Increased Dodge Chance
Increased Magic Defense
Increased Energy Generation

Gameplay Summary

Connie would be an S Tier evasive support, similar to a character who wins fights through control rather than raw damage.

Her main purpose would be:

Making enemies waste attacks
Protecting fragile allies
Disrupting enemy skill rotations
Punishing enemies who rely on single-target attacks

She would not be a traditional tank because she does not survive by having huge HP. Instead, she survives because enemies struggle to interact with her.

Her weakness would be:

Area attacks that ignore targeting
Anti-dodge mechanics
Long fights where her disruption becomes less impactful

Overall, Connie would be one of the strongest defensive utility characters in the game because her ghost abilities translate naturally into an auto battler. She would feel unique without just being "another invisible character."

Squirm is Gardenview's anxious, book-munching centipede-like bibliophile. Under Dandy's Heroes Battle Mania (DHBM) standards, Squirm is a high-tension, high-disruption frontline Tank. Constantly on the verge of a panic attack, his sheer terror and neurotic coping mechanisms—stress-eating ancient tomes, curling into a razor-sharp paper cocoon, and wildly flailing all of his many limbs in a paper-shredding flurry—make him a resilient, clutch protector. While formidable under pressure, he is balanced and mortal: enemy teams with concentrated burst or anti-heal can overwhelm him, but ignoring his panic triggers will unleash FULL BIBLIO-PANIC to turn the tide of defeat into overwhelming victory.

Distressed Delicacy

White Skill (Ultimate)
Damage Type: Physical Damage / Sustain / Crowd Control

Passive: Squirm's stress-eating instincts activate when danger closes in. Whenever Squirm takes damage while below 50% HP, he instinctively devours an ancient volume, restoring 14% Max HP, cleansing 2 debuffs, and gaining a Ward barrier for 3.5s (12s internal cooldown). Whenever Squirm stress-eats, his frantic chewing inspires his allies, granting all allies 8 Energy!

Active: Squirm completely panics and burrows into a massive enchanted tome for 3.0 seconds!
- Gains +30% Damage Reduction, CC Immunity, Disarm, and rapid continuous regeneration (recovering 14% Max HP over the duration).
- While eating, Squirm cannot use Basic Attacks, but he cannot be interrupted or displaced.
- When the feast ends, Squirm violently ejects the book with a Literary Shockwave!
- Deals Physical Damage (2.20x Attack) to all enemies within 240px (+up to 60% bonus damage based on missing HP).
- All enemies hit by the shockwave are Stunned for 1.2s and suffer 25% Armor and Magic Defense shred for 6s.

Please Don't Hurt Me

Green Skill
Damage Type: Shield / Reflect / Team Fortification
Cooldown: 12 seconds

Squirm curls up defensively into a razor-edged paper cocoon, begging to be left alone!
- Gains a paper shield equal to 22% of his Max HP and recovers 2.5% Max HP per second for 4.5 seconds (11.25% Max HP total).
- While the Shield remains active, Squirm reflects 25% of incoming damage back onto attackers as Physical Damage, and attackers are Slowed (-35% Attack Speed and Move Speed) and Weakened (-20% Attack) for 3.5s.
- When the Shield breaks or expires, Squirm unleashes a Spine Shiver burst:
- Taunts all nearby enemies within 220px for 2.5s, forcing them to focus him.
- Grants Squirm and all allies Fortification (+25% Defense and Magic Defense for 6s) and restores 15 Energy to every ally.

Nervous Flailing

Blue Skill
Damage Type: Physical Damage / Interrupt / Lifesteal
Cooldown: 11 seconds

Squirm completely loses his composure, rolling forward and wildly flailing his numerous limbs in a 6-hit flurry of paper slashes!
- Strikes all enemies within a 210px radius 6 times for 48% Attack Physical Damage each.
- Each hit interrupts enemies and knocks them back slightly.
- For every distinct enemy struck, Squirm gains 1 stack of Panic Armor (up to 5 stacks: +5% Defense, +5% Magic Defense, and +2% Damage Reduction for 7s).
- Desperate Siphon: If Squirm is below 50% HP, hits siphon a small amount of HP per enemy struck.
- Final Slam: The 6th strike is a double-limb ground slam that deals 1.25x Attack area damage and Stuns all enemies in the radius for 1.0s.

Bookworm

Purple Skill (Passive)
Damage Type: Permanent Stat Scaling / CC Tenacity / Team Aura

Squirm's peculiar appetite allows him to absorb the wisdom and structural integrity of the texts he consumes.
- Whenever Squirm restores Health through any of his skills or stress-eating, he digests the literary knowledge, permanently gaining 1 stack of 📖 Digestive Erudition for the remainder of battle (up to 10 stacks).
- Each stack permanently grants Squirm +1.2% Max HP, +1.0% Healing Received, and +1.2% Attack.
- Panic Reflex (CC Tenacity): Whenever affected by Crowd Control (Stun, Silence, Freeze, Sleep, Slow, Root), Squirm panics!
- Gains +25% Defense and Magic Defense for the duration of the CC.
- Heals 4% Max HP upon being CC'd (5s internal cooldown).
- Generates Energy 30% faster while under Crowd Control.
- Walking Archive Aura: At maximum stacks (10), Squirm radiates compiled knowledge, reducing incoming damage taken by all allies by 8% and granting all allies +10% Skill Haste.

What Could Go Wrong?

Red Skill (Opener / Climax)
Damage Type: Dual-Threshold Survival / Lethal Defiance / Full Biblio-Panic

Squirm constantly anticipates worst-case scenarios, turning his chronic anxiety into survival contingencies.
- Dual-Phase Panic Thresholds: When Squirm's Health drops below 50% and 25% Max HP, he instinctively panic-eats:
- Cleanses 2 debuffs, restores 14% Max HP, gains a Ward Shield (16% Max HP for 4.5s), and grants 10 Energy to all allies. (Each threshold activates once per battle).

- 📚 FULL BIBLIO-PANIC (Emergency Climax & Lethal Defiance):
- Once per battle, whenever Squirm would suffer lethal damage (or if his HP falls below 12%):
- Squirm devours an entire encyclopedia, restoring 30% Max HP and gaining Invulnerability for 1.5s and CC Immunity for 10 seconds.
- Reduces remaining cooldowns of Please Don't Hurt Me (G) and Nervous Flailing (B) by 50%.
- Enters FULL BIBLIO-PANIC for 10 seconds: gains +30% Attack, +25% Attack Speed, +15% Defense & Magic Defense, and piercing paper slash basic attacks!
- Emits a panic shriek that Taunts all surviving enemies for 3.0 seconds.

Why SS Tier?

Squirm is a premier survival tank and disruptor: he converts enemy pressure and crowd control into escalating defensive stats, team energy generation, and clutch counter-attacks. With dual-threshold panic cleanses, sustained team shields, and a high-stakes Full Biblio-Panic transformation, Squirm anchors the team with authentic DHBM-tier mechanics while remaining fair, mortal, and balanced.

Basic Attack — Signal Beam
Vee fires a quick magic beam. A hit on the Headline adds Signal Interference. Every third hit sends a second pulse through that enemy and into another target. The pulse deals extra damage based on the Headline's Max HP, with a lower cap against bosses. While Live, each Basic Attack fires an extra beam.

White — Spotlight On
Passive: Vee designates the toughest living Tank as the Headline, or the enemy with the most Max HP when there is no Tank. The Headline takes 20% more damage. She changes targets if a tougher Tank arrives or the current Headline falls.
Active: The first focused beam cracks 35% of the Headline's current Shield. Three beams hit the Headline, and the last spills onto the rest of the enemy team. Each focused hit adds Signal Interference.

Green — Audience Surge
An applause cue becomes a magic blast across the enemy team. Vee cracks 25% of the Headline's remaining Shield before a second pulse hits it and adds Signal Interference.

Blue — Commercial Break
Vee cuts the feed to static. Four waves damage every enemy and build Signal Interference. Each stack cuts Magic Defense by 8%; at three stacks, the target also receives 25% less healing. The final wave slows enemies.

Purple — Ratings Chase
Direct damage to the Headline gives Vee one Ratings stack for 8 seconds, up to eight. Each stack grants 4% Magic Attack. At full Ratings, further direct hits fire an extra magic shot that also deals up to 2% of the target's Max HP. Red — Live Broadcast
Once per wave, eight Ratings stacks put Vee Live for 8 seconds. She gains 30% Magic Attack and 25% Attack Speed, and every Basic Attack fires an extra beam. The changeover also sends five magic volleys across the enemy team.
