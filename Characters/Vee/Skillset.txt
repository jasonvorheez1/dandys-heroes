Role: Damage
Position: Backline
Damage Type: Magic
Tier: SS

Vee is a ranged magic attacker built to wear down Tanks. She puts the toughest Tank on camera as the Headline, or the enemy with the most Max HP when there is no Tank. Her beams chip Max HP, crack Shields, and build Signal Interference. Damaging the Headline builds Ratings for a short burst window. She has no team healing, shielding, or damage redirection.

Basic Attack — Signal Beam
Vee fires a quick magic beam. A hit on the Headline adds Signal Interference. Every third hit sends a second pulse through that enemy and into another target. The pulse deals extra damage based on the Headline's Max HP, with a lower cap against bosses. While Live, each Basic Attack fires an extra beam.

White — Spotlight On
Passive: Vee designates the toughest living Tank as the Headline, or the enemy with the most Max HP when there is no Tank. The Headline takes 20% more damage. She changes targets if a tougher Tank arrives or the current Headline falls.
Active: The first focused beam cracks 35% of the Headline's current Shield. Three beams hit the Headline, and the last spills onto the rest of the enemy team. Each focused hit adds Signal Interference.

Green — Audience Surge
An applause cue becomes a magic blast across the enemy team. Vee cracks 25% of the Headline's remaining Shield before a second pulse hits it and adds Signal Interference.

Blue — Commercial Break
Vee cuts the feed to static. Four waves damage every enemy and build Signal Interference. Each stack cuts Magic Defense by 8%; at three stacks, the target also receives 25% less healing. The final wave slows enemies.

Purple — Ratings Chase
Direct damage to the Headline gives Vee one Ratings stack for 8 seconds, up to eight. Each stack grants 4% Magic Attack. At full Ratings, further direct hits fire an extra magic shot that also deals up to 2% of the target's Max HP. This Max HP damage is capped by Vee's Magic Attack; against bosses, it uses 0.8% Max HP and a lower cap. The shot has a short cooldown. Vee also gains 12% Magic Penetration.

Red — Live Broadcast
Once per wave, eight Ratings stacks put Vee Live for 8 seconds. She gains 30% Magic Attack and 25% Attack Speed, and every Basic Attack fires an extra beam. The changeover also sends five magic volleys across the enemy team.

The revised kit uses Vee's existing spotlight, audience, commercial, and live broadcast visuals. Its attack sounds reuse the game's beam, laser, static, impact, and Vee ability cues.
