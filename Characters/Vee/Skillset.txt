Role: Tank / Support / Battlefield Director
Tier: A Tier

Vee is the charismatic television host of Gardenview. She is analytical, competitive, and always wants everything to run efficiently. In Dandy's World, she specializes in gathering information through Skill Checks, making her one of the best characters at exposing Twisteds and helping her team. Rather than turning her into a generic shield tank, this kit revolves around directing the battlefield like a live television production, marking priority targets, exposing weaknesses, and protecting allies through superior planning. She stands on the front line because she controls the pace of combat, not because she is the toughest character.

Spotlight On

White Skill
Damage Type: Magic Damage

Passive: Vee is constantly broadcasting the battle.

Every few seconds she identifies the enemy contributing the most to their team's damage as the Headline.

Only one enemy may be the Headline at a time.

All allies gain increased Accuracy and deal bonus damage against the Headline.

Whenever the Headline casts a skill, Vee records valuable data, granting all allies Energy.

Active: Vee floods the battlefield with blinding studio lights.

All enemies take Magic Damage.

The current Headline is Interrupted, briefly Blinded, and loses a percentage of their current Energy.

If the Headline is defeated while marked, Vee immediately selects a new one and all allies recover Health.

Audience Support

Green Skill
Damage Type: Healing

Vee calls for audience participation.

Cheers from the crowd strengthen nearby allies.

The three allies with the lowest Health recover HP over several seconds while gaining Armor and Magic Defense.

Any overhealing becomes a Shield.

If an ally already has a Shield, they instead gain increased Energy Generation until the effect expires.

Commercial Break

Blue Skill
Damage Type: Buff

Vee pauses the flow of battle for the enemy team.

Enemy cooldowns recover more slowly for several seconds.

Meanwhile, allies regenerate Energy faster, gain increased Skill Haste, and become immune to Energy drain effects.

Whenever an ally casts a skill during Commercial Break, they recover a small amount of Health.

Studio Production

Purple Skill
Damage Type: Armor
Magic Defense

Vee's production crew is always one step ahead.

Whenever an ally damages the Headline, Vee gains a stack of Production Value.

Each stack increases her Armor and Magic Defense.

At maximum stacks:

Vee redirects a portion of all damage dealt to nearby allies onto herself.
Damage redirected this way is reduced.
Whenever Vee survives redirected damage, she grants the protected ally a burst of Energy.

Production Value is only lost if Vee is defeated.

Season Finale

Red Skill
Damage Type: Armor
Magic Defense
Max HP

The battlefield becomes Vee's studio.

At the start of combat, every enemy is analyzed.

Enemies gradually build Exposure whenever:

They attack.
They cast skills.
They defeat an ally.

At maximum Exposure, they become the Headline automatically.

Headline enemies:

Deal reduced damage.
Cannot become Invisible or Untargetable.
Receive reduced healing.
Generate less Energy.
Take increased damage from all allies.

Whenever a Headline enemy is defeated, Vee immediately:

Restores Energy to every ally.
Refreshes all active Shields.
Reduces every ally's cooldowns.
Permanently increases her Armor and Magic Defense for the rest of the battle.

Once per battle, if every other ally is defeated, Vee enters Live Broadcast for 12 seconds.

During Live Broadcast:

She cannot be Interrupted.
Her basic attacks become ranged energy blasts.
Spotlight On activates every few seconds without consuming Energy.
Every surviving enemy is treated as the Headline.
Why A Tier?

Vee excels in coordinated teams by identifying the most dangerous opponent and turning the entire team against them. She offers reliable protection, cooldown support, and sustained utility, but her effectiveness depends on the battle developing over time. Unlike SS-tier controllers that can instantly reshape the battlefield or S-tier supports with universally powerful healing, Vee rewards planning and target prioritization, making her a uniquely strategic A-tier tank/support rather than a straightforward defensive wall.
