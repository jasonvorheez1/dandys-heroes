Gigi

Role: RNG Support
Tier: S Tier

Gigi is a living gachapon who loves collecting things. In battle, she produces random items from her collection and gives them to her allies. The items vary in usefulness, making her unpredictable, but her ability to provide exactly the kind of utility a team needs gives her an extremely high ceiling.

Item Pool

The important part is that these should be actual Dandy's World items, not generic RPG objects.

For example, Gigi could randomly produce things such as:

Bandage: Restores Health.
Chocolate: Restores Energy.
Valve: Increases Attack Speed.
Gumball: Provides a random temporary buff.
Bottle: Restores Health over time.
Speed Candy: Increases Movement Speed.
Extraction Candy: Increases Skill Haste or Energy generation.
Pop Pack: Provides a stronger temporary combat effect.

The exact pool could expand as more items are added to Dandy's World.

Why S Tier?

Gigi becomes an item based RNG support, rather than simply a character who randomly rolls buffs.

That makes her much more faithful to both sides of her character. She is a gachapon, so producing random things is fundamental to her identity, while her tendency to collect and hoard things gives her a reason to have an enormous inventory of possible effects.

Her biggest strength is adaptability. A dedicated healer will heal more consistently, and a dedicated buffer will provide stronger predetermined buffs, but Gigi can potentially provide healing, Energy, defense, offense, mobility, or utility depending on what she happens to produce.

The RNG also isn't completely meaningless. Her AI can prioritize items based on what her allies currently need, so she isn't just randomly making the team better or worse.

S Tier: Gigi is an unpredictable but exceptionally versatile support who turns Dandy's World items themselves into her arsenal.
