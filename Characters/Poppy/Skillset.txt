Poppy

Role: Tank / Team Protection / Control
Tier: B Tier

Poppy turns danger into cover for her friends. Her bounces interrupt enemies, her bubbles shelter the team, and falling low gives her a second wind. Ralph's Disney Heroes Battle Mode kit informed the skill rhythm; Poppy keeps her own bubble identity and team role. See Ralph-notes.md.

White — Panic Pop
Passive: Taking a direct hit gives Poppy Buoyant, raising Movement Speed and Evasion. Dodging while Buoyant restores Energy.
Active: Shield the lowest-HP teammate, then bounce five times through enemies. Each hit deals Physical Damage, knocks back, and Slows. The final pop Stuns enemies around its target.

Green — Bubble Bounce
Poppy gives herself a large bubble and teammates smaller Bubble Shelters with Speed Up. Casting Silences nearby enemies briefly. When her bubble breaks or expires, it pushes enemies back, gives her Buoyancy, refreshes allied shelters, and restores Energy to nearby allies.

Blue — Pop Shock
Poppy bursts outward, damaging, knocking back, and inflicting Off Balance on enemies. The strongest enemy caught is Stunned. Allies protected by her shields echo the burst around themselves, extending its reach without dealing duplicate hits.

Purple — Air Filled
Direct damage grants Air Pressure. When one of Poppy's shields breaks on an ally, she gains two stacks and Energy. Each stack raises Max HP, Shield Strength, and Knockback Resistance. At ten stacks she cannot be displaced and takes less damage. Once per wave below 30% HP, she gains a rescue bubble and restores 22% Max HP over seven seconds; repeated Red skill activations heal less.

Red — The Biggest Bubble
Poppy starts with a large shield and shares smaller shields with the team. Air Filled can trigger three times per wave. Each trigger cleanses her, grants brief control immunity and Speed Up, and lengthens her Stuns and Silence for seven seconds. The first time she would be defeated, she becomes an invulnerable giant bubble and slowly heals. When it bursts, she returns with HP, damages and pushes back nearby enemies, shields and energizes the team, and keeps a Movement Speed boost for the rest of battle.
