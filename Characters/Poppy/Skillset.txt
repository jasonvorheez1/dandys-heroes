Poppy

Role: Tank / Self-Sustain
Tier: D Tier

Poppy is a cheerful bubble Toon who relies on her natural bounce and resilience to survive dangerous situations. She is energetic and optimistic, but her lightweight body makes her better at avoiding danger than standing toe-to-toe with powerful enemies.

In an RPG auto battler, Poppy becomes a beginner-friendly defensive character who specializes in self-preservation, temporary protection, and disrupting enemies through her bouncing movements. She can survive longer than expected, but she lacks the team protection and battlefield control of stronger tanks.

The Biggest Bubble	Red	Max HP
Shield	At the start of battle, Poppy gains a protective layer of air around herself.

The first time Poppy would be defeated, she instead becomes a giant bubble.

While in bubble form:

• She cannot attack.
• She cannot be damaged.
• She slowly restores HP.

When the bubble pops, Poppy returns to battle, damaging and pushing back nearby enemies.

Afterward, she gains increased Movement Speed but reduced Attack for the rest of the battle.
Why D Tier?

Poppy is a durable character, but most of her strengths are focused on surviving herself rather than helping her team. She can avoid damage, recover from dangerous situations, and disrupt enemies, but she does not provide the protection of stronger tanks.

Her bubble body makes her surprisingly difficult to remove, but stronger tanks offer more value through ally protection, enemy control, or stronger defensive effects.
