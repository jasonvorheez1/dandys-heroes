# Ralph reference notes for Poppy

Sources: [Disney Heroes: Battle Mode Wiki — Ralph](https://disneyheroesbattlemode.fandom.com/wiki/Ralph) and [Disney Heroes: Battle Mode Database — Ralph](https://wakkatu.github.io/dhero/en/hero/RALPH.htm). Both describe Ralph as a frontline Tank whose control skills lead into a low-HP recovery and a Red skill that strengthens that recovery.

| Ralph's DHBM skill | What gives it impact | Poppy adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| White: Wreck It | A nearby area hit Stuns several enemies. | Panic Pop ends its bouncing sequence with a small area Stun. Her first action also shields a vulnerable teammate. |
| Green: Shouting Match | A separate area Silence interrupts enemy skills. | Bubble Bounce briefly Silences enemies near Poppy as it shelters the team. |
| Blue: Grand Slam | A heavier, focused hit locks down one enemy longer. | Pop Shock Stuns the strongest enemy caught, while its main wave applies Off Balance and echoes from protected allies. |
| Purple: Big Duty | Crossing 30% HP triggers healing over time once per wave. | Air Filled adds a once-per-wave second wind below 30% HP: immediate healing, regeneration, and a rescue bubble. |
| Red: Heavy Handed | The Purple rescue repeats with diminishing healing; each trigger cleanses and strengthens Ralph's control. | The Biggest Bubble lets Air Filled trigger three times per wave with smaller later heals, cleanses Poppy, grants brief control immunity and speed, and extends her Stuns and Silence. Her giant-bubble comeback remains her signature. |

Ralph's friendship disks further emphasize longer Stuns and healing when enemies are Stunned. Those are useful examples of his control-and-survival loop, but Poppy's core kit uses her bubbles for that loop rather than copying the disk effects.
