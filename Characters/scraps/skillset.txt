Scraps

Tier: SS Tier
Role: Tank / Control / Utility Support

Scraps is a creative and energetic Toon who loves making art and crafts. Her long stretchy arms allow her to bring her creations to life, manipulate the battlefield, and protect her friends. She does not win fights through brute force, but through clever inventions, unpredictable attacks, and constantly changing the battlefield around her.

Why SS Tier?

Scraps is SS-tier because she combines battlefield control, protection, and scaling durability into one character. Her creativity gives her a unique way to influence fights: instead of simply attacking enemies, she changes the battlefield with her crafts.

She can protect allies like a tank, disrupt enemies like a controller, and weaken opponents so her team can finish them. Her weakness is that she is not the highest damage dealer, but her ability to constantly create advantages makes her one of the strongest support tanks.
