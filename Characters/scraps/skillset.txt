Scraps

Tier: SS Tier
Role: Damage

Scraps is an unstoppable, relentless paper-craft predator. Armed with razor-sharp cardboard shredders, a crushing cup flail, and weighted yarn snares, she hurls herself aggressively across the battlefield with Crafty Grapple, dragging an expanding Craft Circle that continuously shreds armor and tears through enemy ranks. Striking Frayed enemies drives her into a relentless frenzy of blinding scissor slashes and ricocheting paper shrapnel.

Name	Rarity	Damage Type	Description
Crafty Grapple	White	Physical Damage	Passive: Every third Basic Attack unleashes her current handmade craft with heightened ferocity: Cardboard punctures with heavy Armor Penetration and Frays, Cup flails a group while cracking shields, and Yarn deals damage, Frays, and heavily slows. Scraps' attacks cannot be Dodged by Frayed enemies, and striking Frayed foes builds Relentless Shred (+Attack Speed, +Move Speed, and double-strikes at max stacks). Active: Scraps launches a high-velocity grapple toward the fray (or to an endangered ally) while completely Invulnerable and Untargetable. On landing, her Craft Circle slams down in an expanding shockwave that pulls enemies inward, deals heavy Physical Damage, applies Frayed, and unleashes an immediate 3-hit scissor claw flurry. If any foe dies shortly after, Scraps refunds 45 Energy and resets Whatever Works.
Whatever Works	Green	Physical Damage	Rapidly cycles her craft arsenal (Cardboard Claws -> Cup Flail -> Yarn Ball) and grants Craft Adrenaline (+30% Attack Speed, +25% Move Speed). Instantly strikes with the chosen craft: Cardboard pierces ignoring 50% Armor with bonus damage against Frayed foes; Cup Flail sweeps a wide arc dragging enemies inward and concussing them; Yarn Ball fires a ricocheting snare that roots enemies and applies Frayed.
Craft Class Crash	Blue	Physical Damage	Scraps spins into a manic hurricane of cut paper, scissors, and flying origami shurikens, striking all nearby enemies 3 times in rapid succession. Each wave deals Physical Damage, gains bonus damage for each Frayed stack, refreshes Frayed, and shreds enemy Armor. Scraps gains Crowd Control immunity and 25% damage reduction during the spin.
Craft Circle	Purple	Physical Damage / Attack Speed	The Craft Circle around Scraps is an active shredder zone. Every second, enemies inside take Physical Damage, take increased damage from Scraps, and gain Frayed stacks. Cardboard mode shreds 25% Armor, Cup mode increases circle damage by 50% and pulls foes inward, and Yarn mode slows Move Speed and Attack Speed by 35%. Scraps gains +6% Attack per nearby enemy (up to 5 enemies), while nearby allies gain stacking Attack Speed.
Every Piece Counts	Red	Physical Damage / Craft Circle	Each skill cast permanently expands Craft Circle for the battle and triggers Paper Frenzy (+25% Critical Damage and +25% True Damage on craft and circle hits). Whenever an enemy inside the circle is defeated or suffers a critical strike, they erupt into razor paper shards that deal heavy Physical Damage to all nearby foes and spread Frayed. Slain foes refund 25 Energy and extend Paper Frenzy.

Why SS Tier?

Scraps embodies the premier relentless physical DPS archetype: high-frequency attacks, armor-shredding area control, gap-closing invulnerability, and cascading resets. By aggressively chaining her crafts and keeping enemies trapped in her growing Craft Circle, she relentlessly blends through enemy formations with non-stop scissor flurries and explosive paper shard detonations.
