Ginger

Role: Healer / Buffer / Team Sustain
Tier: A Tier

Ginger is a supportive character who keeps her team alive through kindness and preparation. She is not a frontline fighter or a burst healer. Instead, she slowly improves her team's ability to survive through healing, morale boosts, and carefully prepared treats.

Her biggest strength is making a team stronger over a long battle.

Baked With Care!

White Skill
Damage Type: Healing

Passive: Ginger keeps track of injured allies.

Whenever an ally falls below 50% HP, Ginger gains a stack of Concern.

Each stack increases her next healing effect.

Active: Ginger gives one of her own Hearts to an ally, restoring a large amount of their HP. At maximum Perfect Decoration, the next use heals the whole team and consumes the decorations.

The healing is extremely powerful but reduces Ginger's own HP.

Ginger cannot use this skill if she is at critical HP.

If this skill saves an ally from defeat, both Ginger and that ally gain Encouraged:

Increased Defense.
Increased Energy Generation.
Increased Resistance to Crowd Control.
Decorated Treats

Green Skill
Damage Type: Buff

Ginger shares her decorated cookies with the team.

All allies gain Sweet Support for several seconds.

All allies also gain Freshly Baked, a status effect that cleanses one negative status when applied and increases Crowd Control Resistance.

Sweet Support provides:

Increased Attack Speed.
Increased Skill Recharge.
Increased Healing Received.

The ally with the lowest HP receives an additional Shield.

Frosting Care

Blue Skill
Damage Type: Healing / Cleanse

Ginger sends decorated cookies to the whole team.

All allies are healed and receive Freshly Baked.

Freshly Baked cleanses one negative status when applied and increases Crowd Control Resistance.

The ally with the lowest Health also receives a Shield.

Little Improvements

Purple Skill
Damage Type: Passive

Ginger's attention to detail helps everyone.

Whenever Ginger heals or buffs an ally, she gains a stack of Perfect Decoration.

Each stack increases:

Healing Power.
Buff Duration.
Energy Generation.

At maximum stacks, Ginger's next support skill affects the entire team.

A Recipe For Everyone

Red Skill
Damage Type: Healing
Max HP

Before battle begins, Ginger prepares treats for everyone.

At the start of combat:

All allies gain a small HP increase.
All allies gain increased Healing Received.
The weakest ally receives an extra Shield.

During battle, whenever an ally drops below 25% HP, Ginger automatically gives them a Last Bite.

Last Bite:

Restores HP.
Removes one negative status effect.
Gives a temporary damage boost.

After activating, Ginger becomes Determined:

Increased Healing Power.
Increased Defense.
Reduced skill cooldowns.
Why A Tier?

Ginger is an extremely reliable support because she directly prevents teams from collapsing. Her healing can completely reverse dangerous situations, and her buffs make every teammate perform better.

However, she is not S Tier because her support is reactive rather than controlling. She cannot manipulate enemies like Vee, protect the whole battlefield like Astro, or completely change positioning like Scraps.

She is an A-tier team backbone: a character who makes everyone around her better, but still needs a strong team to reach her full potential.
