# Teagan and Yzma reference notes

## Dandy's World: who Teagan is

The [Dandy's World Wiki's Teagan page](https://dandys-world-robloxhorror.fandom.com/wiki/Teagan) records her introduction card and dialogue: Teagan is a gracious tea host who remembers other Toons' preferred drinks. She accommodates guests, including a straw for Goob, water for Finn, and a different kind of gathering for Yatta. She is calm, elegant, and attentive. She cares about etiquette and keeping Ichor off her boa, but those details do not make her a punitive or contemptuous character. Her pronouns are she/they.

Her Dandy's World ability, **Tea Time**, spends **75 Tapes to heal Teagan herself by one Heart** and has a long cooldown. The [Dandy's World Wiki](https://dandys-world-robloxhorror.fandom.com/wiki/Teagan) notes that the cost was previously 100 Tapes before an update. The RPG kit translates the 75 Tape payment and self heal directly; team shields, Haste, and control are battle game adaptations of her hospitality.

## Yzma's Disney Heroes: Battle Mode kit

The [DHBM Yzma database](https://wakkatu.github.io/dhero/en/hero/YZMA.htm) lists:

| Skill | Mechanic | Teagan adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Push Your Luck (White) | A potion grows stronger the longer Yzma mixes it, with a chance to backfire. | Tea Time's protection grows with recent basic attacks as the tea steeps. Teagan's careful preparation has no harmful backfire. |
| Llama Drama (Green) | One enemy takes magic damage and a long Stun. | A Seat at the Table interrupts one dangerous enemy and shields a guest. Teagan does not transform anyone. |
| Scary Beyond All Reason (Blue) | An area Scare slows the opposing side's plans. | A Quieter Table applies Drowsy to enemies while Fortifying allies. Teagan calms the room instead of frightening it. |
| Alchemy (Purple) | Every two potions produce an extra control effect. | Every two basic attacks, Remembered Orders serves a useful cup to the ally who needs it most. |
| Wardrobe Change (Red) | Basic attacks splash, spreading the Purple effect. | A Cup for Everyone lets basic attacks splash and spreads Drowsy when a prepared cup is served. |

## Design guardrails

- Keep Tea Time's **75 Tapes for Teagan's own healing** visible and mechanically real.
- Let her support teammates through shields, pacing, and thoughtful cups. Other Toons' tastes inform the tone of the kit; no single tea is presented as universally correct.
- Preserve her poise and boundaries without turning her into Yzma or a scolding authority figure.
- Keep the kit's RPG control effects framed as pauses and calm, not intimidation, curses, poison, or punishment.
