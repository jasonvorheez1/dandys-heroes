Rodger

Role: Backline Control
Tier: C Tier

Rodger does not perform Basic Attacks. Instead, he continuously investigates enemies from the backline, building Research on them and using his findings to disrupt their abilities. He is weak when enemies are not yet studied, but becomes increasingly troublesome against targets he has had time to investigate.

Research

Research is not a buff or debuff. It is simply information Rodger accumulates about an enemy.

This is important because it keeps the mechanic faithful to Rodger's actual identity. He isn't magically weakening people because he "studied" them. He's watching them, figuring them out, and then using that knowledge to interfere with what they do.

Why C Tier?

Rodger has an unusual strength curve.

At the beginning of a fight, he's relatively unimpressive. He doesn't attack normally, his damage contribution is minimal, and his strongest control effects require him to spend time researching enemies.

But once he has thoroughly investigated an important enemy, he can become extremely annoying for that specific target.

He's especially good against:

Skill dependent enemies
High Energy characters
Powerful backline enemies
Enemies with dangerous buffs
Bosses with predictable powerful Skills

He's much worse against:

Large groups of weak enemies
Extremely fast battles
Enemies that don't rely on Skills
Teams that can quickly reach his backline

That makes C tier feel appropriate. He isn't bad, but he's highly specialized and requires setup.

Most importantly, this gives Rodger an identity that isn't just "detective who throws magnifying glass."

He's the weird backline character who spends the fight watching the enemy instead of attacking them, gradually filling out his case file until he knows exactly when to shut them down.
