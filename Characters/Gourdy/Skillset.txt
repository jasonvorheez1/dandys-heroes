Role: Control / Support
Damage Type: Magic

Why I'd change the previous version

The previous kit made Gourdy too much like a random status effect character. That's not really what makes him Gourdy.

His actual gimmick is much cleaner:

Gourdy gives you something random.

That's what Trick or Treat actually does.

So I would build the entire RPG kit around Treats and Tricks, rather than giving him arbitrary random attacks.

His RNG should have a purpose

For example, Gourdy uses Trick or Treat and gets:

+25% Attack Speed

Another time:

+25% Defense

Another:

+25% Mana Regeneration

You don't control what you receive, but every result is useful.

His Purple skill makes the RNG more interesting by potentially turning one result into two. That gives you the "gambling" aspect without making him frustrating.

His control is where the B Tier comes from

Gourdy's direct damage should be awful.

His Magic Attack can be intentionally low, and Pumpkin Toss should mostly exist as a delivery mechanism for his control effects.

His real value is:

Random stat buffs + random enemy disruption + excellent Movement Speed support.

And I'd make his control effects very strong when they happen, because otherwise RNG characters can become useless.

For example, a lucky Pumpkin Toss could:

Silence + Slow

while an unlucky one might only:

Slow

That's where his RNG creates excitement.
