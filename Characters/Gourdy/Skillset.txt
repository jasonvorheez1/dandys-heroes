Gourdy

Tier: B
Role: Control / Support
Damage Type: Magic

Gourdy's core kit works with any team and with or without a Trinket. His parents and Trinkets add their own synergies, but none is required to activate a skill's full combat effect.

Basic Attack — Pumpkin Candy Toss
Deals light Magic Damage. Each hit has a 35% chance to give the lowest-HP ally a random Treat or apply a random Trick to the target. Treats buff one useful stat; Tricks weaken one enemy stat.

White — Spooky Treat Extravaganza
Passive: Basic Attacks can deliver Treats or Tricks. Active: Gourdy showers the team with random Treats, heals each ally for 85% of his Magic Attack plus 12% of his Max HP, and gives each 18 Energy. Nearby enemies take 85% Magic Attack damage, receive a random Trick, and are rooted for 1.5 seconds. Treats last 9 seconds.

Green — Sugar Rush & Harvest Cheer
At battle start, grants teamwide Speed Up and Skill Haste for 7 seconds. A Treat or Gourdy's critical hit triggers a shorter teamwide rush with 6 Energy. Reaching full Mana or casting White refreshes the rush and Shields Gourdy and the lowest-HP ally for 16% of Gourdy's Max HP. Trinkets can add benefits, but do not enable this skill.

Blue — Mega Pumpkin Patch Slam
Throws a giant Jack-o'-Lantern at an enemy, prioritizing a Hug Line target. The main hit deals 175% of Gourdy's Magic Attack (125% base ratio × 1.4), restores 25 Energy to Gourdy, Stuns for 1.5 seconds, roots for 2 seconds, and applies two random Tricks. The impact breaks a Hug Line. Nearby enemies take splash damage and a Trick. All effects occur whether or not either parent is in the party; their presence adds a family themed visual flourish.

Purple — Trickster's Harvest
A Treat or Trick has a 25% base chance to add a second, different effect; Cooldown Reduction or Energy Generation improves this chance. Once every 10 seconds, if an ally falls below 40% HP, Gourdy grants them a Shield equal to 26% of his Max HP and cleanses 2 debuffs.

Red — The Great Halloween Miracle
Gourdy rolls three Treats each time he grants one and chooses the most useful for that ally. The two unchosen options become random Tricks on enemies for 5 seconds. Allies below 50% HP favor defensive Treats. The team gains +15% Energy Generation for the fight.

Design intent
Gourdy remains a low-damage, high-utility support built around unpredictable but useful Treats, enemy Tricks, team speed, and emergency protection. His full Slam and rescue tools are part of his own kit; a Memory Locket, Candy Wrapper, or Holloway teammate is a bonus rather than an unlock condition.
