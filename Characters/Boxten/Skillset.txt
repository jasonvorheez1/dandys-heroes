Boxten

Role: DPS / Consistent Attacker
Tier: D Tier

Boxten is a quiet and reserved music box Toon who prefers staying around familiar faces. His actual ability, Wind-Up, increases his Extraction Speed depending on how many Toons are alive, making him a team-oriented character rather than a fighter.

For an RPG auto battler adaptation, Boxten becomes a steady but unimpressive DPS. His music box design gives him a unique rhythm-based fighting style, but he lacks the explosive damage, survivability, or utility needed to compete with stronger damage dealers. His strength comes from consistency, not power.

Why D Tier?

Boxten is a reliable damage dealer, but he struggles to stand out. His rhythm mechanic rewards consistent attacking, but it takes time to build and does not provide the same explosive payoff as stronger DPS characters.

Compared to Rudie, who can snowball through speed and momentum, or other high-tier carries who have stronger burst or team effects, Boxten is simply too dependent on maintaining his setup.

His greatest strength is reliability. He does not completely fail, but he rarely dominates.

D Tier: A steady music-box fighter who can keep a rhythm going, but lacks the impact needed to compete with stronger DPS characters.
