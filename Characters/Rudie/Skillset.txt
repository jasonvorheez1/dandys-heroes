Rudie

Tier: SS Tier
Role: DPS / Assassin / Momentum Carry

Rudie is a hyperactive reindeer Toon who relies on speed, confidence, and overwhelming pressure. Rather than being a simple attacker, Rudie becomes stronger the longer he maintains his momentum. His gameplay revolves around charging through enemies, building speed stacks, and turning movement into damage.

Why SS Tier?

Rudie is an SS-tier DPS because he is a true scaling carry. Unlike characters who have one burst window, Rudie becomes more dangerous the longer he stays active. His Momentum mechanic rewards aggressive play and allows him to snowball fights.

His weaknesses are clear: if enemies can consistently disable him or prevent him from building momentum, his performance drops heavily. However, when supported by tanks or characters who protect him from crowd control, Rudie can completely overwhelm enemy teams.

SS Tier: A high-speed carry who turns constant movement into unstoppable damage.
