Why D Tier?

Eggson is a perfectly functional DPS, but his kit is intentionally built around utility and consistency rather than raw damage. His ability to Inspect enemies gives him a little team value, while his cane attacks provide respectable sustained damage.

His biggest problem is that his strongest characteristics in Dandy's World are not combat abilities. His real strength is his experience, his extraction utility, and his ability to help get Machines working. His 20% Machine completion ability is genuinely useful in the original game, but there is no direct equivalent in an RPG combat system.

So rather than inventing an overly powerful combat gimmick, this adaptation makes him an old-fashioned, straightforward fighter with a little utility attached.

D Tier: A surprisingly capable elderly fighter who knows exactly what he's doing, but lacks the damage output and specialized combat utility of stronger DPS characters.
