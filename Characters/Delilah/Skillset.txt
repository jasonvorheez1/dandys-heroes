Delilah Keen

Role: Control / Summoner
Tier: SS Tier

Delilah is a clinical observer who turns the battlefield into a living experiment. She marks dangerous targets, releases the right Twisted sample for the formation, and rewards careful control with stronger specimens.

Name	Rarity	Damage Type	Description
Controlled Experiment	White	True Damage / Summon	Passive: Delilah releases a formation-selected Twisted at the start of battle. Crowd-controlled enemies become Test Subjects, and Delilah and her samples cannot be Dodged by marked targets.

Active: releases another sample up to the active cap and immediately performs its signature action.
Unstable Sample	Green	True Damage / Debuff	Injects the highest-Energy enemy for True Damage and applies Contamination. Test Subjects spread the contamination to a nearby enemy. Cleansing Contamination causes a brief Stun, and contaminated defeats heal Delilah's weakest active sample.
Containment Procedure	Blue	Control / Cleanse	Orders every active Twisted to surround the highest-Attack enemy, applying Contained. Delilah and her samples gain Sterile Protocol, which cleanses one negative status when received and improves Crowd Control Resistance.

When Containment ends, every sample strikes the target. If Delilah has no samples, she Stuns the target instead.
Clinical Observation	Purple	Skill Haste / Defense	Test Subjects casting or suffering Crowd Control feeds Delilah Energy and Skill Haste. The first time each marked target falls below half Health, her active samples are permanently reinforced. A sample defeated by a Test Subject becomes a Failed Variable for the next release.
Further Testing Required	Red	True Damage / Control	Delilah can maintain a third sample. Sample defeats leave Ichor Spills that damage enemies and heal samples. Sustained Crowd Control marks new Test Subjects and helps recharge Controlled Experiment. Once per wave, a surviving sample intercepts Delilah's lethal damage.
