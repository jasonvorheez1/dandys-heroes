Looey

Role: DPS / Speed Fighter
Tier: D Tier

Looey is a cheerful balloon Toon and part of a circus troupe alongside Yatta and Blot. His personality revolves around performing, making people laugh, and hiding his nervousness behind his energetic personality. His defining ability, Heart of Helium, makes him become faster as he loses Hearts, turning damage into mobility.

For this RPG auto battler adaptation, Looey becomes a high-risk speed-based DPS. His gimmick is that he gets stronger when injured, but unlike higher-tier carries, he struggles to convert that speed into consistent damage. He is exciting and can snowball, but his reliance on missing HP keeps him unreliable.

Why D Tier?

Looey has a fun and unique mechanic, but it is difficult to make work consistently in an auto battler. His entire kit revolves around being injured, which creates a dangerous balance problem: if he is healthy, he is average; if he is weak, he becomes powerful but also much easier to defeat.

Compared to higher-tier DPS characters like Rudie, who can build momentum while staying aggressive, Looey has to intentionally put himself at risk before reaching his strongest state.

His speed is impressive and his comeback potential is real, but he lacks the damage consistency, survivability, and team utility needed for higher ranks.

D Tier: A risky speed-based DPS who can have explosive moments, but struggles to compete with reliable carries.
