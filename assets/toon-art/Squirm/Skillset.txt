Role: Tank / Disruptor / Survivor
Tier: SS Tier

Squirm is Gardenview's anxious, book-munching centipede-like bibliophile. Under Dandy's Heroes Battle Mania (DHBM) standards, Squirm is a high-tension, high-disruption frontline Tank. Constantly on the verge of a panic attack, his sheer terror and neurotic coping mechanisms—stress-eating ancient tomes, curling into a razor-sharp paper cocoon, and wildly flailing all of his many limbs in a paper-shredding flurry—make him a resilient, clutch protector. While formidable under pressure, he is balanced and mortal: enemy teams with concentrated burst or anti-heal can overwhelm him, but ignoring his panic triggers will unleash FULL BIBLIO-PANIC to turn the tide of defeat into overwhelming victory.

Distressed Delicacy

White Skill (Ultimate)
Damage Type: Physical Damage / Sustain / Crowd Control

Passive: Squirm's stress-eating instincts activate when danger closes in. Whenever Squirm takes damage while below 50% HP, he instinctively devours an ancient volume, restoring 14% Max HP, cleansing 2 debuffs, and gaining a Ward barrier for 3.5s (12s internal cooldown). Whenever Squirm stress-eats, his frantic chewing inspires his allies, granting all allies 8 Energy!

Active: Squirm completely panics and burrows into a massive enchanted tome for 3.0 seconds!
- Gains +30% Damage Reduction, CC Immunity, Disarm, and rapid continuous regeneration (recovering 14% Max HP over the duration).
- While eating, Squirm cannot use Basic Attacks, but he cannot be interrupted or displaced.
- When the feast ends, Squirm violently ejects the book with a Literary Shockwave!
- Deals Physical Damage (2.20x Attack) to all enemies within 240px (+up to 60% bonus damage based on missing HP).
- All enemies hit by the shockwave are Stunned for 1.2s and suffer 25% Armor and Magic Defense shred for 6s.

Please Don't Hurt Me

Green Skill
Damage Type: Shield / Reflect / Team Fortification
Cooldown: 12 seconds

Squirm curls up defensively into a razor-edged paper cocoon, begging to be left alone!
- Gains a paper shield equal to 22% of his Max HP and recovers 2.5% Max HP per second for 4.5 seconds (11.25% Max HP total).
- While the Shield remains active, Squirm reflects 25% of incoming damage back onto attackers as Physical Damage, and attackers are Slowed (-35% Attack Speed and Move Speed) and Weakened (-20% Attack) for 3.5s.
- When the Shield breaks or expires, Squirm unleashes a Spine Shiver burst:
- Taunts all nearby enemies within 220px for 2.5s, forcing them to focus him.
- Grants Squirm and all allies Fortification (+25% Defense and Magic Defense for 6s) and restores 15 Energy to every ally.

Nervous Flailing

Blue Skill
Damage Type: Physical Damage / Interrupt / Lifesteal
Cooldown: 11 seconds

Squirm completely loses his composure, rolling forward and wildly flailing his numerous limbs in a 6-hit flurry of paper slashes!
- Strikes all enemies within a 210px radius 6 times for 48% Attack Physical Damage each.
- Each hit interrupts enemies and knocks them back slightly.
- For every distinct enemy struck, Squirm gains 1 stack of Panic Armor (up to 5 stacks: +5% Defense, +5% Magic Defense, and +2% Damage Reduction for 7s).
- Desperate Siphon: If Squirm is below 50% HP, hits siphon a small amount of HP per enemy struck.
- Final Slam: The 6th strike is a double-limb ground slam that deals 1.25x Attack area damage and Stuns all enemies in the radius for 1.0s.

Bookworm

Purple Skill (Passive)
Damage Type: Permanent Stat Scaling / CC Tenacity / Team Aura

Squirm's peculiar appetite allows him to absorb the wisdom and structural integrity of the texts he consumes.
- Whenever Squirm restores Health through any of his skills or stress-eating, he digests the literary knowledge, permanently gaining 1 stack of 📖 Digestive Erudition for the remainder of battle (up to 10 stacks).
- Each stack permanently grants Squirm +1.2% Max HP, +1.0% Healing Received, and +1.2% Attack.
- Panic Reflex (CC Tenacity): Whenever affected by Crowd Control (Stun, Silence, Freeze, Sleep, Slow, Root), Squirm panics!
- Gains +25% Defense and Magic Defense for the duration of the CC.
- Heals 4% Max HP upon being CC'd (5s internal cooldown).
- Generates Energy 30% faster while under Crowd Control.
- Walking Archive Aura: At maximum stacks (10), Squirm radiates compiled knowledge, reducing incoming damage taken by all allies by 8% and granting all allies +10% Skill Haste.

What Could Go Wrong?

Red Skill (Opener / Climax)
Damage Type: Dual-Threshold Survival / Lethal Defiance / Full Biblio-Panic

Squirm constantly anticipates worst-case scenarios, turning his chronic anxiety into survival contingencies.
- Dual-Phase Panic Thresholds: When Squirm's Health drops below 50% and 25% Max HP, he instinctively panic-eats:
- Cleanses 2 debuffs, restores 14% Max HP, gains a Ward Shield (16% Max HP for 4.5s), and grants 10 Energy to all allies. (Each threshold activates once per battle).

- 📚 FULL BIBLIO-PANIC (Emergency Climax & Lethal Defiance):
- Once per battle, whenever Squirm would suffer lethal damage (or if his HP falls below 12%):
- Squirm devours an entire encyclopedia, restoring 30% Max HP and gaining Invulnerability for 1.5s and CC Immunity for 10 seconds.
- Reduces remaining cooldowns of Please Don't Hurt Me (G) and Nervous Flailing (B) by 50%.
- Enters FULL BIBLIO-PANIC for 10 seconds: gains +30% Attack, +25% Attack Speed, +15% Defense & Magic Defense, and piercing paper slash basic attacks!
- Emits a panic shriek that Taunts all surviving enemies for 3.0 seconds.

Why SS Tier?

Squirm is a premier survival tank and disruptor: he converts enemy pressure and crowd control into escalating defensive stats, team energy generation, and clutch counter-attacks. With dual-threshold panic cleanses, sustained team shields, and a high-stakes Full Biblio-Panic transformation, Squirm anchors the team with authentic DHBM-tier mechanics while remaining fair, mortal, and balanced.
