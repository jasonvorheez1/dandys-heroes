Dyle

Role: DPS
Tier: SS Tier

Dyle is an exceptionally consistent DPS who controls the battlefield through his train and precise attacks. Rather than relying on flashy supernatural abilities, he fights with the same strict efficiency he applies to his work. His attacks establish routes across the battlefield, allowing him to repeatedly strike enemies who fail to move out of the way.

Active: Dyle signals the train and a train rapidly travels across the battlefield along a straight route, dealing heavy Physical Damage to every enemy it passes through. Enemies hit are briefly knocked aside.
