Blot is a silent, mime-themed ink blob who plays in the Circus Troupe. Mechanically, he excels as a distractor and survivalist using his signature active ability—spawning a Blot Jr. decoy to draw enemy threat away from himself and his teammates.In an auto-battler setting, Blot functions as an A-Tier Frontline Controller.
