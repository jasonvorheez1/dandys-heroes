Bassie

Role: Support / DPS
Tier: A Tier

Bassie always keeps her basket stocked with useful supplies, believing that being prepared is the best way to help her friends. She eagerly shares whatever she has with her teammates, and when her basket is overflowing, she turns the remaining supplies into surprisingly effective attacks.

Why A Tier?

Bassie is a proactive support that rewards preparation. She constantly keeps her basket stocked, automatically helping struggling teammates while converting any extra Supplies into offensive pressure. Unlike Cocoa, whose BonBons continuously accelerate an entire team, Bassie focuses on resource management, deciding whether her Supplies are used to rescue allies or fuel her own attacks.

Her versatility makes her valuable in many team compositions, but because her strongest effects depend on maintaining a healthy stock of Supplies, she lacks the overwhelming consistency and tempo control that define SS-tier supports. She excels as a flexible hybrid who always has something useful ready.
