Why SS Tier?

The important distinction is triage.

Sprout doesn't just throw out a big team Heal every so often. His passive awareness lets him constantly identify who needs help, while Baked Sweets makes his healing naturally prioritize the most injured ally. That's a direct adaptation of Overprotective and Baked Sweets rather than an invented plant or magic gimmick.

His high Stamina and Movement Speed also become relevant through Stay Close. Canonically, Sprout has 5 star Stamina and 4 star Movement Speed, so making him capable of quickly repositioning to an endangered ally fits much better than making him a stationary healer.

And I've Got You is where his SS tier comes from. Sprout is fundamentally the character who is constantly watching everyone else's condition and getting involved when someone is in trouble. His personality is explicitly described as overprotective and caring, sometimes to the point of being pushy.

I also wouldn't give him magical plant attacks just because he's a strawberry. The food is the mechanic. His healing comes from baking and giving things to people, which is much more faithful to what actually defines him. His own dialogue reinforces that baking is a major part of his identity, with lines about things being fresh out of the oven and making sure everyone is okay.

This gives him a very different identity from the other healers:

Sprout: reactive emergency healer who watches everyone's HP
Cocoa: item based temporary buffs
Cosmo: more direct self sacrifice and healing
Astro: broader team support
Sprout: "You're hurt. I'm dealing with that now."
